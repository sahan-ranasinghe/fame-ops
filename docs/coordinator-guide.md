# Fame Fleet: a guide for Coordinators

You are a **Coordinator**. You keep the records right and help run the day: drivers, vehicles, their documents and
photos, office accounts, issues, attendance, requests and tasks. You cannot approve requests, change the fleet
settings, import projects or change an admin's account. If something needs one of those, ask a Fleet admin or an
Admin.

You can work from a computer (the website, **https://fame-fleet.pages.dev**) or from a phone (the same address opens the
phone version under **/m/**, or the Fame Fleet app with **Office** chosen at sign-in). The screens below use the
website's names; the phone has the same screens under its tabs and **More**.

## 1. Sign in

1. An Admin creates your account and sends you a one-time link by WhatsApp.
2. Open the link, choose a password of at least 12 characters, and save it. You land on **Overview**.
3. Next time: open the website, type your e-mail and password, and sign in.
4. Your name is at the bottom of the left menu. Click it for **My profile**, **Settings** and **Sign out**. On a phone,
   these are at the top of **More**.
5. On **My profile**, add your mobile number. Your photo is added from the phone: **More** → your name → **Change photo**.

## 2. Register a driver

1. Left menu → **Drivers** → **Add driver**.
2. Fill in the name, the employee code, a username (for example `kasun.s`) and the mobile number.
3. A 6-digit PIN is made for the driver. **Write it down or send it to the driver now**: it is shown once.
4. Open the driver's page to add the licence number and expiry, the licence photos and the emergency contact.
5. The driver signs in to the Fame Fleet app with the username and the PIN.

If a driver forgets the PIN: open the driver → **New PIN**, and send them the new one.

## 3. Register a vehicle

1. Left menu → **Vehicles** → **Register vehicle**.
2. The number plate (for example `WP CAB-4521`), the make and model, the type, the seats and the odometer now.
3. If it is a rental: the rental company (add one from the same form), the monthly rent and the km allowance.
4. Save. The vehicle's page opens.

## 4. Add documents and photos

1. Open the vehicle (**Vehicles** → the plate).
2. **Documents**: add the revenue licence, the insurance and the emission certificate. For each one, take or choose the
   photo and type the expiry date. The vehicle's expiry dates follow.
3. **Photo**: add a photo of the vehicle.
4. A document that is about to expire shows in orange, an expired one in red, and both appear under **Reminders**.

Driver photos and licence photos are added on the driver's page in the same way.

## 5. Add an office user

1. Left menu → your name → **Settings** → **Users** → **Add office user** (on a phone: **More** → **Office users**).
2. Type the name and the e-mail.
3. Pick a template (it ticks the boxes; you can change them after):
   - **Requester**: asks for vehicles and follows their own trips. Most office staff need only this.
   - **Viewer**: sees the fleet, changes nothing.
   - **Dispatcher**: assigns and changes tasks.
   - **Coordinator**: what you have.
4. **Create account**, then send the one-time link it shows by WhatsApp.

You can only give permissions you have yourself. You cannot give **Approve** or **Users** (Admin) to anyone, and you
cannot change your own permissions. To change an Admin's account, ask an Admin.

## 6. Request a vehicle, assign a task

- **Request a vehicle** (for someone in the office): website **Requests** → **New request**; phone **Home** →
  **+ New trip** → **Request a vehicle**. Add the stops, the day and one time (when to leave, or when to arrive at a
  stop), then send. A Fleet admin approves it.
- **Assign a task** (a driver and a vehicle for a trip): website **Tasks** → **Assign a task**; phone **Home** →
  **+ New trip** → **Assign a task**. Add the stops, the day and one time, pick the driver (their usual vehicle comes
  with them), and send.
- Because you do not approve, **a task you assign waits for a Fleet admin's OK** before the driver gets it. You see it
  under **Requests** as waiting until it is approved.
- The planner suggests the other times in grey. You can change them; it never blocks you.

## 7. Today's attendance

1. Left menu → **Drivers board** → **Attendance** (on a phone: the **Drivers** tab → **Attendance**).
2. Every driver is in one of four groups: **On duty** (with the time they started), **Not started yet** (with the time
   they usually start), **Finished for today** (started and finished times) and **On leave**.
3. Use **Call** or **WhatsApp** on a row to check on someone who has not started.

## 8. Handle an issue

1. Left menu → **Issues** (the number beside it is how many are open).
2. Open an issue and read what happened (photos are in it for a vehicle problem).
3. **Take** it (it shows your name), add a **note** about what you did, then **Resolve** it when it is fixed, or
   **Dismiss** it if it is not a real problem.
4. A vehicle problem that needs a garage: set it to **in the garage**, so nobody books that vehicle.

## 9. What you must never do

- Never share your password or a driver's PIN with anyone except that driver.
- Never create an account for someone who should not have one, and never give more permissions than the person needs
  (most people need only **Requester**).
- Never change the details of a trip that is under way unless the Fleet admin asks you to.
- Never switch off an account or a driver without being asked: it stops them at once.
- Never type personal information (phones, addresses) into notes where it is not needed.
- Everything you change is recorded under your name in the **Activity log** that the Admins can see.
