# Loud Fame Fleet notifications on an Android phone (Chrome)

For Palitha, and anyone who approves requests or assigns tasks on an Android phone. Hotfix 2, item B.

Fame Fleet can tell you about something new in two ways:

1. **The phone's notification** (push). It works when the Fame Fleet page is closed and when the phone is locked.
   Android decides how loud it is. Sections A to D below set it up.
2. **The page's own sound and banner.** Fame Fleet plays one of these only while its page is open: urgent (a task to
   approve, a task that needs a driver), alert (a request decided, a task declined), soft (on the way, arrived) and
   alarm (a critical issue). See section E.

Menu names differ a little between phone brands and Android versions. Where a step does not match your phone, look
for the nearest name.

## A. Install Fame Fleet on the home screen

1. Open **Chrome** and go to **https://fame-fleet.pages.dev**. Sign in.
2. Tap Chrome's menu (the three dots, top right), then **Add to Home screen** (on some phones **Install app**), then
   **Install**.
3. From now on open Fame Fleet from its new icon on the home screen, not from a Chrome tab.

An installed Fame Fleet gets its own entry in Android's app list, so its notifications can have their own sound.

## B. Turn on notifications in Fame Fleet

1. In Fame Fleet tap **More**, then **Settings**, then **Notifications**.
2. Under **This browser**, tap **Turn on in this browser**. When the phone asks, tap **Allow**.
3. Under **Tell me when**, tick the events you want, for example a request comes in or a task needs a driver.
4. Under **Browsers with notifications on**, this phone should now be listed.

## C. Make the notifications loud

1. Open Android **Settings**, then **Apps** (on some phones **Apps & notifications**).
2. Find **Fame Fleet** in the list. If it is not there, use **Chrome** instead (Fame Fleet then sends its notifications
   through Chrome).
3. Tap **Notifications** and make sure notifications are **on**.
4. Open the notification category for the site. It is named **Fame Fleet** or **fame-fleet.pages.dev**. With Chrome it
   is under **Sites**.
5. In that category set:
   - **Importance** (or Behaviour): **Urgent**, or **Make sound and pop on screen**.
   - **Sound**: choose a loud ringtone. Do not leave it on **Silent**, and do not pick a quiet default.
   - **Pop on screen** (or Floating notifications, or Banners): **on**.
   - **Lock screen**: **Show all content**.
   - **Vibrate**: **on**.
   - **Override Do Not Disturb** (or Priority, or Allow during Do Not Disturb): **on**.
6. Battery: in the same app page, open **Battery** and choose **Unrestricted** (or **Allow background activity**).
   Without this, some phones (OPPO, realme, Xiaomi, vivo) hold notifications back until you open the phone.

Some brands put these in their own places:

- **Samsung**: Settings, then Notifications, then App notifications, then Fame Fleet; tap the categories for the sound
  and Pop-up style. For Do Not Disturb: Settings, then Notifications, then Do not disturb, then App notifications, add
  Fame Fleet.
- **Xiaomi, Redmi, POCO**: Settings, then Notifications & Control centre, then App notifications, then Fame Fleet. Turn
  on **Floating notifications** and **Lock screen notifications**.
- **OPPO, realme (ColorOS)**: Settings, then Notifications & status bar, then Manage notifications, then Fame Fleet.
  Turn on **Banners**, **Lock screen**, **Ringtone** and **Vibrate**. Also allow **Auto launch** for Fame Fleet in the
  app's settings.

## D. Keep the banner when the screen is on

Leave **Pop on screen** (step C.5) on. Then a new notification shows as a banner at the top of the screen while you use
the phone, with its sound, instead of only an icon in the status bar.

## E. The page's own sounds (only while Fame Fleet is open)

- They play only while the Fame Fleet page is open on the phone or the computer. A closed page, or a phone that has
  closed the page in the background, stays quiet: then only the phone's notification from sections B to D tells you.
- A browser keeps a page silent until you tap it once. After opening Fame Fleet, tap **Click to turn on sounds** at the
  top of the page, or tap anywhere on it. The strip then goes away.
- **Settings, then Notifications, then Sounds while Fame Fleet is open**: turn the sounds on or off, set their volume,
  and tap **Test sound** to hear all four (urgent, alert, soft, alarm), or **Play** beside one.
- On a phone these sounds follow the phone's **media volume**, not the ringtone volume. Keep the media volume up.
- Each new item also shows a banner on the page (who, what, **Open**, and a close button) until you open or close it.
  The bell beside the issues bell keeps every item, with **Mark all read**.

## F. Check that it works

1. Lock the phone.
2. Ask Sahan, or someone with a requester account, to send a test request.
3. The phone should ring with the sound you chose in step C and show the notification on the lock screen.
4. Open Fame Fleet: the item is in the notification list (the tray icon at the top) with the number of unread items.
